Meetings run your organization. Run them well.

Every organization lives or dies in its meetings — the budget gets set, the hire gets made, the plan gets approved, or it all drifts for another week. Run the Room takes one meeting type at a time and breaks it into the three parts that decide whether it works: how you plan it, how you run it, and how you follow up.

Hosts Alex and Jordan work through a rotating circuit of the rooms where real decisions happen — boardrooms, hiring committees, project kickoffs, town halls — with the people who lead them as guests.

The Format

1

Planning

Who needs to be in the room, what the agenda owes them, and the preparation that makes the meeting decided before it starts.

2

Running

Opening, pacing, disagreement, decisions — the chair's craft, taken apart while the clock is running.

3

Follow-Up

Notes, owners, deadlines, and the quiet work after the meeting that decides whether anything actually happened.

Be a Guest

We interview the people who run real rooms: executives, board chairs, superintendents, founders, committee chairs, and the operators whose meetings carry their organizations. One conversation, about how you plan, run, and follow up the meetings that matter in your world.

Guests are invited to close with a short plug for their organization — recorded by you, in your own voice (a phone voice memo is fine), and used exactly as you give it. Interview recordings are likewise used as-is: your voice, unaltered, on the record.

To be considered, write to runtheroom@outlook.com with your name, your role, and the meeting you run that you'd most like to talk about.
